Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

What Wisdom Fairy is

A private Slack app used only by our own team to turn chosen Slack messages into quote tiles.

What data it uses

  • Only messages a team member explicitly selects with the Make it wisdom ✨ shortcut: the message text, the author's Slack display name, the time it was sent, and a link to the message.
  • The Slack name of the person who shared it.

It never reads other messages or channels.

Google data

The app uses the Google Drive scope drive.file only. That means it can access just the files it created itself: one Drive folder with the tile images and one Google Sheet that lists them. It can't see, read or change any other file in the Google account.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

Tiles are posted in our Slack workspace and saved in our team's own Google Drive and Google Sheet. The app keeps no other database. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI training.

Removing data

Any team member can delete a tile from Slack, the Drive folder or the Sheet at any time.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
