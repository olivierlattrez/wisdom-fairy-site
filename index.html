✦ WISDOM FAIRY ✦

Little pink tiles for big team wisdom

Wisdom Fairy is a small private Slack app for our own team. When someone says something wise or funny, a teammate picks Make it wisdom ✨ on that message and the app turns it into a quote tile in our #wisdom channel.

Every tile is also saved to a Google Drive folder and Google Sheet owned by our team, so it outlives Slack's message history.

It isn't offered to the public and has no sign-up.
